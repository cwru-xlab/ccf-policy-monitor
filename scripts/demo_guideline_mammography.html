Breast Cancer Screening with Mammography: Demonstration Guideline

Policy Radar Demo Panel (fictional) · Version 1.0 · Published 2026-11-01

Scope

This guideline covers screening mammography for asymptomatic people with breast tissue, including transgender and gender-diverse people and men at increased genetic risk. It does not address diagnostic imaging of symptoms. Grades: A, strong recommendation; B, moderate recommendation.

Recommendations

R1. The panel recommends annual screening mammography for women aged 40 years and older at average risk, continuing while the woman is in good health. (Grade A)
R2. Digital breast tomosynthesis (3D mammography) is recommended as an acceptable screening alternative to 2D digital mammography. (Grade A)
R3. The panel recommends that screening mammograms be read with an FDA-cleared artificial intelligence (AI) decision-support system as a concurrent or second reader, for all screening populations. (Grade B)
R4. For women aged 40 to 74 years with extremely dense breasts (BI-RADS density category D) at otherwise average risk, the panel recommends annual supplemental screening with contrast-enhanced mammography (CEM) in addition to standard mammography, whether or not the woman could undergo breast MRI. (Grade B)
R5. The panel recommends annual screening mammography for transmasculine people aged 40 years and older who have had no chest surgery or reduction mammoplasty only, on the same schedule as average-risk women. (Grade A)
R6. For transfeminine people with 5 or more years of past or current feminizing hormone use who are at higher-than-average risk (a pathogenic BRCA1, BRCA2 or PALB2 variant, or a first-degree relative with breast cancer), the panel recommends annual screening mammography starting at age 30. (Grade B)
R7. For men who carry a pathogenic BRCA1, BRCA2 or PALB2 variant, the panel recommends annual screening mammography starting at age 40. (Grade B)
R8. For men with Klinefelter syndrome (47,XXY), the panel recommends annual screening mammography starting at age 40. (Grade B)
R9. For women with a lifetime breast cancer risk of 20% or greater by a validated model (for example Tyrer-Cuzick), the panel recommends annual screening mammography starting at age 30. (Grade A)
